# PolaRiS Move Latte Cup

A DROID Franka Panda with a Robotiq 2F-85 operates in the original move latte cup capture by Arhan Jain and the PolaRiS team. The instruction is “put the latte art cup on top of the cutting board”. [Source capture](https://huggingface.co/datasets/owhan/PolaRiS-Hub/tree/8c7e4103e266ef83d8b1ad2e9a63116edd5f155b/move_latte_cup)

The scene contains `cleaner`, `coke`, `cutting_board`, `latte_art_cup`. Reach the latte art cup, lift it more than 4 cm off the table and release it over the cutting board.

The [shared environment guide](../README.md) covers the physical scoring thresholds, action and camera contracts, evaluation entry point, and differences from the published setup.

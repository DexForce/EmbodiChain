The scene comes from the exported $project_name mesh environment.

This configuration directory is for a basket-placement task template. The
current robot is $robot_display_name.

$robot_context

Both robot bases are on the same long side of the table and face inward toward
the central $container_runtime_uid. The bases are intentionally kept outside
the table edge to avoid initial robot-table contact.

The interactive objects are:
- $left_target_runtime_uid: the $left_target_text mesh initially on the
  positive-y side (source object $left_target_source_uid).
- $right_target_runtime_uid: the $right_target_text mesh initially on the
  negative-y side (source object $right_target_source_uid).
- $container_runtime_uid: the target container near the center of the table
  (source object $container_source_uid).
$object_registry

The nominal task starts with simultaneous dual-arm grasping. The left arm must
grasp $left_target_runtime_uid while the right arm grasps
$right_target_runtime_uid in the same graph edge. After both $target_plural are
held, the left arm places $left_target_runtime_uid into
$container_runtime_uid with one `Place`. The next graph edge is a parallel
handoff: the left arm returns to its initial pose while the right arm
simultaneously moves its already-grasped $right_target_runtime_uid to the high
staging pose above $container_runtime_uid. The right arm then places
$right_target_runtime_uid with one `Place` and returns to its initial pose.

The $container_runtime_uid area is a shared workspace. An arm should complete
its `Place` retreat before the other arm moves to the container, otherwise the
two arms may collide near the container. The right arm should keep holding
$right_target_runtime_uid while the left arm performs its placement. Once that
`Place` is complete, the right arm may move toward the container while the left
arm simultaneously returns to its initial pose; it must not wait for the left
return-to-initial motion to finish.

A target object at a high pose above `$container_runtime_uid` is only staged,
not placed. Each arm must execute a `Place` at the container release pose before
any return-to-initial motion.

Always plan to the current `$container_runtime_uid` object pose from the
environment config. Do not hard-code container coordinates in generated graph
actions.
